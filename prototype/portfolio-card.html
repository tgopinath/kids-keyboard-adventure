Kids Keyboard Adventure

A keyboard game for children aged 3 to 10, guided by KeyBot, a robot who talks, cheers and changes his face as the child plays. Meet Your Keyboard explains every key aloud, from A to F12. Find the Key, Alphabet Adventure, Number Keyboard and a Memory Challenge teach where keys live; Falling Letters, Bubble Pop, Letter Catcher, Word Builder and Treasure Hunt make practice a game; Typing Challenge goes from single letters to sentences with live accuracy and speed. Smart Difficulty moves up at 90%, stays at 70–89% and steps down with hints below 70%, and missed keys come back more often. Stars and coins buy KeyBot hats, colours and new worlds. A parent dashboard behind a maths question shows time, accuracy, speed and the hardest keys, and nothing leaves the device. Every sound and the music are made in the browser.

  • StackReact 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Lucide, Web Audio, Web Speech
