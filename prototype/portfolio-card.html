Kids Keyboard Adventure

A keyboard game for children aged 3 to 10, guided by KeyBot, a small robot who explains, encourages and celebrates. Meet Your Keyboard explains every key aloud, from A to F12. Find the Key, Alphabet Adventure, Number Keyboard and a Memory Challenge teach where keys live; Falling Letters, Bubble Pop, Letter Catcher, Word Builder and Treasure Hunt turn practice into play; Typing Challenge goes from single letters to sentences with live accuracy and speed. Smart Difficulty moves up at 90%, stays at 70–89% and steps down with hints below 70%, and missed keys come back more often. The interface is calm and neutral; only the on-screen keyboard keeps chunky, colour-coded keys a child can tell apart. A parent dashboard behind a maths question shows time, accuracy, speed and the hardest keys, and nothing leaves the device.
